About Me

I am a Computer Engineering student at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines (Main), currently diving into Big Data as an elective. My goal is to build a rock-solid foundation as a Data Engineer, with a specific focus on environmental and social applications.

My journey is driven by a desire to give back. While participating in social and environmental initiatives, a turning point occurred during an organization event where I learned how to pitch ideas. This sparked a vision of how I could leverage data engineering to create a tangible, positive future for our communities. I deeply believe in the philosophy of being "a light in the darkness"—using technology to care, protect, and provide hope.

Beyond data pipelines, I have a strong passion for electronic circuits and thoroughly enjoy building hands-on projects that bridge hardware and software.
